    Keys

    → / ←
    Leave the title page for the buzzers / back to the title page (buzzers locked)
    Space
    Open buzzers for the next question, or lock them (on the title page: go to the buzzers)
    C
    Correct: adds points and locks the buzzers
    W
    Wrong: deducts points and ends the question (no pass). If “pass to the next team” is ticked, it goes to the next team in line instead
    N
    Hand the question to the next team in line, no points (manual override)
    1–4
    Buzz for a team from this keyboard (1 = A, 2 = B, 3 = C, 4 = D), if a phone fails
    M
    Manual mark: choose a team and give +10, −5, +5 or any number, with no buzz needed (works on the title page too)
    U
    Undo the last score
    T / Y
    Start or pause the round clock / reset it (only when a round clock is set)
    J
    Show or hide the join codes
    F
    Full screen
    P
    Show or hide this panel
